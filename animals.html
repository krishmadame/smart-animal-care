Educational Animal Health Guidance

The care information provided below is general educational material designed for good farm husbandry. It does not replace a veterinary examination. If any livestock exhibits acute illness, respiratory distress, or cessation of rumination, consult a certified veterinarian immediately.

Dairy & Draught

Dairy Cow (गाय / गोवंश)

Bos taurus / Bos indicus

Cattle form the backbone of rural dairy economies. Whether indigenous breeds like Gir, Sahiwal, and Kankrej or high-yielding crossbreds, cows require consistent feeding schedules, clean bedding, and careful udder hygiene to sustain optimal milk production.

Gestation Period: 280 - 285 Days
Daily Water: 60 - 90 Liters
Normal Body Temp: 38.5°C (101.5°F)
Key Productivity: Milk & Organic Manure

Key Care Focus:

  • Grooved concrete or rubber-matted floors to prevent hoof slip and lameness.
  • Post-milking teat dip with 0.5% iodine solution to shield teat canals from environmental bacteria.
  • Routine deworming at onset of monsoon and post-monsoon.
High Fat Dairy

Water Buffalo (भैंस)

Bubalus bubalis

Renowned for producing dense milk rich in butterfat (7-8%), water buffaloes such as the Murrah, Nili-Ravi, and Mehsana are robust converters of coarse crop residues. They have fewer sweat glands and dark skin, demanding dedicated heat mitigation.

Gestation Period: 305 - 315 Days
Daily Water: 80 - 110 Liters
Normal Body Temp: 38.0°C (100.5°F)
Key Productivity: High Fat Milk & Draught

Key Care Focus:

  • Provide clean wallowing ponds or water splashing twice daily in hot summers.
  • Inspect wider teat orifices for mud contamination before and after milking.
  • Watch for silent heat signs during morning and evening milking sessions.
Small Ruminant

Domestic Goat (बकरी)

Capra hircus

Affectionately known as the farmer’s companion or "poor man’s cow", goats are prolific, drought-hardy browsers. Famous Indian breeds including Jamunapari, Black Bengal, and Sirohi supply nutritious milk and meat with minimal starting capital.

Gestation Period: 145 - 152 Days
Daily Water: 5 - 10 Liters
Normal Body Temp: 39.0°C (102.2°F)
Key Productivity: Meat, Milk & Kids

Key Care Focus:

  • Keep shed floor dry and elevated on slats; goats cannot tolerate damp, muddy floors.
  • Regularly trim overgrown hooves to forestall contagious foot rot.
  • Strict adherence to PPR and Enterotoxemia (ET) vaccination calendars.
Wool & Meat

Domestic Sheep (भेड़)

Ovis aries

Sheep graze close to the ground and excel at utilizing stubble, arid grasses, and hill pastures. Breeds such as the Marwari, Deccani, and Nellore furnish wool, mutton, and rich penning manure, but require protection from wet fleece hypothermia.

Gestation Period: 145 - 150 Days
Daily Water: 4 - 8 Liters
Normal Body Temp: 39.0°C (102.5°F)
Key Productivity: Mutton, Coarse Wool & Manure

Key Care Focus:

  • Annual shearing prior to hot months and winter to avoid heat distress and fleece soiling.
  • Flock dipping in antiparasitic solution 2-3 weeks post-shearing for tick and mite control.
  • Avoid copper-rich cattle supplements to protect sheep from fatal copper toxicity.
