Proactive Husbandry

6 Core Pillars of Preventive Care

“Simple habits today can support healthier livestock tomorrow.”

🏡

1. Clean & Ventilated Shelters

Stagnant moisture and ammonia vapors cause respiratory lesions and mastitis. Ensure shed roofs are at least 10-12 feet high with ridge vents. Build floor with a 1:40 slope towards external drainage channels to keep standings dry.

🧼

2. Strict Farm Hygiene & Sanitation

Remove cow dung twice daily. Dust shed floors with slaked lime (चूना) weekly to neutralize pathogens. Install a 10% lime or potassium permanganate footbath at the farm entrance to disinfect visitor boots and vehicle tires.

💉

3. Timely Vaccination Schedules

Vaccines build specific antibodies against deadly epidemics. Never vaccinate an animal that is running a high fever, severely emaciated, or heavily infested with internal worms, as immune response will fail.

🩺

4. Seasonal Deworming & Health Exams

Deworm all livestock at least 3-4 times annually (specifically pre-monsoon in May and post-monsoon in October). Rotate dewormer molecules (Albendazole, Fenbendazole, Ivermectin) to prevent parasite drug resistance.

💧

5. Continuous Clean Drinking Water

Ensure continuous access to potable water in shaded troughs. Stagnant green-scummed water harbors cyanobacteria, leptospirosis, and liver fluke snails. Scrub water troughs with bleaching powder every 10 days.

👁️

6. Early Attention & Quarantine Isolation

Observe livestock behavior daily during morning milking. A drop in milk yield, dull ears, dry muzzle, or lack of rumination are early warnings. Immediately isolate any sick or newly purchased animal in a separate quarantine pen.
